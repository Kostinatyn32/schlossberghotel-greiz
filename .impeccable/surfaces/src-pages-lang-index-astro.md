## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The hotel is the clear starting point for a small town worth exploring. Refuse the generic hotel-photo-and-card grid; orient guests through place, rooms, events and arrival.

**OWN-WORLD:** Deep evergreen map ground, chalk linework, a restrained brass route accent and warm paper reading surfaces. Compact uppercase wayfinding labels pair with a clear sans display face. Square edges and thin rules carry structure.

**STORY:** Visitors recognize Greiz, see the hotel as their base, choose a room or event route, then continue to booking, enquiry or arrival details.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A warm-paper header holds the supplied hotel logo, concise route links and one booking button. Below, a full-width evergreen hero places a two-line title and two actions at left, with an original schematic Greiz map filling the right. A three-point fact rail follows.

**FORM:** Third visual direction shown in the user's three-option reference, “Stadt / town guide”, expanded across German and English home, hotel, rooms, events, Greiz and contact routes. User selection supersedes the earlier guestbook direction. seed: none (unavailable)
