New map and room images are original illustrations; neither represents documentary hotel photography. The maps are not to scale.

## Hotel

**DE headline:** Ein Haus mitten in Greiz.  
**EN headline:** A hotel in the heart of Greiz.

The hotel is centrally located at the foot of the Upper Castle. It has 31 rooms, including five suites, two family rooms and one 45 m² wheelchair-accessible room. Guest facilities described on the current hotel site include a breakfast buffet, a lounge, a terrace and a lift. The current site says the rooms were extensively modernised in 2017; confirm whether this detail is still useful before publication.

## Rooms

**DE headline:** Finden Sie den Raum für Ihre Reise.  
**EN headline:** Find the room that suits your stay.

Show six room types without prices: Designzimmer / Design Room (24–26 m²); Design Suite (larger than a Design Room; no published area); Klassikzimmer / Classic Room (22–24 m²); Klassik Suite / Classic Suite (44–64 m²); barrierefreies Zimmer / accessible room (45 m²); Exklusiv Suite / Exclusive Suite (no published area).

Keep equipment details with the relevant type only. The accessible room is described as wheelchair accessible, with a large step-free shower and an accessible washbasin; a parking space may be reserved on request. Do not imply that every hotel space is fully accessible.

**Action:** `Verfügbarkeit prüfen / Check availability`, linking to the official online booking system.

## Events

**DE headline:** Raum für Begegnungen.  
**EN headline:** A place to come together.

The accessible Saal am Schlossberg has 225 m² and is used for celebrations and business events. The current site lists a lectern, Ultra HD projector, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, modern kitchen and sound equipment. The terrace has 65 m². Regional caterers may support the food and drink planning. The official pages do not publish a guest capacity; do not invent one.

**Action:** `Event anfragen / Ask about an event`, opening a pre-addressed email to the hotel's public contact address. Do not imply a submitted form or automatic availability check.

## Explore Greiz

**DE headline:** Die Stadt vor der Tür. Die Natur gleich weiter.  
**EN headline:** The town outside. The countryside just beyond.

The town's official hotel copy highlights the Upper Castle, historic buildings, Art Nouveau houses, museums and Schlosspark. The Weiße Elster valley and wooded hills offer nearby walking and cycling opportunities. The Göltzschtalbrücke is another excursion in the wider Vogtland region. Keep journey times and distances out until they are checked.
