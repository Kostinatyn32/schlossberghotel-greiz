# Schlossberghotel Greiz — verified source notes

Research date: 13 October 2026. Source: the company's current public website.

## Hotel and rooms

- Central Greiz location, at the foot of the Upper Castle.
- 31 rooms in total, including 5 suites, 2 family rooms, and 1 accessible room.
- Rooms face the courtyard, the city, or the castle.
- Design room: 24–26 m²; flat-screen TV, Wi-Fi, desk, chair, shower, hair dryer, cosmetic mirror; baby bed on request.
- Classic room: 22–24 m²; located on the top floor; twin or double beds; flat-screen TV, Wi-Fi, desk, chair, shower, hair dryer, cosmetic mirror; baby bed on request.
- Design suite: larger than design rooms; two lockable rooms, sofa bed, two TVs, Nespresso machine, kettle, Wi-Fi, desk and chairs; no published size found.
- Classic suite: 44–64 m²; sleeping/living rooms with up to four sleeping places, two lockable rooms, sofa bed, two TVs, Nespresso machine, kettle, Wi-Fi; baby bed on request.
- Accessible room: 45 m²; wheelchair accessible, large step-free shower, accessible washbasin, sofa bed; a parking space can be reserved on request.
- Exclusive suite: king-size bed, open-plan bath in the living/sleeping area, Nespresso machine, minibar/fridge, electric fireplace, bathrobes and additional listed amenities; no published size found.
- Check-in from 15:00; check-out by 11:00. Rates may have separate booking/cancellation conditions.
- The hotel page describes a breakfast buffet, coffee/tea service, a lounge and a roof terrace.
- General hotel facilities also include a lift, terrace, shoe-cleaning machine, outdoor smoking area, and lobby drinks vending machine.

## Events

- Accessible Saal am Schlossberg: 225 m²; weddings, anniversaries, birthdays, galas, receptions, marketing events, business meetings, workshops, seminars, forums, presentations and talks are listed.
- Event-page equipment: lectern, Ultra HD projector, air conditioning, Wi-Fi, modern kitchen and sound equipment.
- The hotel mentions lounge/bar space, a 65 m² terrace and regional caterers.
- A separate overview page lists a bar/dance area as 270 m², a small hall as 45 m² and an atrium as 230 m². Verify room names/measurements with the hotel before presenting these as a site plan.
- Venue capacity (people) is not published in the pages reviewed.
- Parking directions conflict: the general arrival page says Neuer Weg; the hall page mentions Siebenhitze/Hohe Gasse. Use only the general hotel arrival direction for now and request hotel confirmation for event visitors.

## Greiz and arrival

- The official site identifies the Upper Castle, castles, Art Nouveau houses, museums, Schlosspark, Weiße Elster valley and surrounding wooded hills.
- Hiking and cycling routes are promoted in the surrounding Vogtland; the Göltzschtalbrücke is also presented as a nearby attraction.
- The underground garage is below the hotel; from it guests can use the lift to reach the hotel.
