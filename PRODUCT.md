# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: standalone Astro static site, using the existing Astro toolchain in the parent workspace so this hotel site stays isolated from other brands in the repository.

## Users

- Visitors planning a stay in Greiz who need to compare rooms, understand the hotel's location and amenities, and book.
- People and organizations looking for a venue for a meeting or event who need accurate room and service information before contacting the hotel.

## Product Purpose

Present Schlossberghotel Greiz and direct visitors to its established online booking system or to the hotel for event enquiries. Success means visitors can quickly find the relevant hotel information and continue to the right booking or contact route.

## Positioning

The hotel combines a central location in Greiz with lodging, suites, an accessible room, and a flexible event venue in one property. Do not claim market exclusivity or unsupported quality rankings.

## Operating Context

The hotel serves leisure and business stays as well as celebrations, meetings, workshops, presentations, and receptions. Its official website offers German and English navigation. Room reservations continue in the hotel's external booking system.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Build a German and English multi-page website with home, hotel, rooms, events, surroundings, and arrival/contact information.
- Keep the hotel name and the supplied original logo. No other imagery or interface assets from the original site may be reused.
- Link accommodation calls to action to `https://buchung.schlossberghotel-greiz.de/onlinebuchung/views/onlinebuchung.php?cds_id=1`.
- Use only verified room, venue, location, amenity, contact, and arrival information. Do not invent room rates, venue capacities, guest reviews, or exact event access instructions.
- Confirmed address: Marienstr. 1-5, 07973 Greiz. The general arrival page says the underground garage entrance is on Neuer Weg; the event hall page gives a different access route. Keep the general arrival instruction separate from event-specific parking pending confirmation.
- The source says check-in is from 15:00 and check-out is by 11:00.

## Brand Commitments

The user explicitly requires using only the logo from the existing Schlossberghotel Greiz website. The existing hotel name and business facts remain authoritative; the visual identity and remaining website presentation are to be redesigned.

## Evidence on Hand

- Original logo copied from the hotel's homepage into `assets/schlossberghotel-logo.png`.
- Source facts and page links are recorded in `research.md`; references point to the official hotel website.
- No customer reviews or verified rating text were supplied for reuse. Do not fabricate them.
- Authentic hotel, room, event and local-area photographs are now sourced from the hotel’s own public website. Original photo URLs and source pages are recorded in `assets/photo-sources.json`.
- Do not label a room type as photographed unless the source photo is documented for that room; no image is assigned to room types without a matching source photo.
- The home and Greiz hero use a generated town panorama to restore the selected city-guide direction. It is explicitly labeled as an illustration, never as documentary photography or a navigation map.

## Product Principles

- Make both accommodation booking and event enquiries easy to find.
- Keep facts accurate and traceable to the official source.
- Help visitors understand access, accessibility, rooms, and event facilities before they commit.
- Keep booking in the hotel's established booking system.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The hotel lists one 45 m² wheelchair-accessible room with step-free shower and accessible washbasin, and says guests can use the lift from the underground garage. Present those details accurately and do not imply the whole hotel is fully accessible.
